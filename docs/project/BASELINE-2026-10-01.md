## 3. Major implemented capabilities

### Shared DXF tooling

- ASCII DXF header and entity parsing.
- Geometry support for `LINE`, `CIRCLE`, and `ARC`.
- Layer-aware rendering and manufacturing-layer conventions for L, V, and V1 data.
- Unsupported-entity reporting and duplicate-line detection.

### CosmicDesigner

- centimeter-based material and geometry model, with a default `300 × 300 cm` material and `0.2 cm` thickness;
- Flat Designer with outer contour, bend-line, cut/hole rendering, selection highlighting, and hole tools;
- W/H Section Designers with bend creation, dimensions, editable distances, Bent Mode, thickness rendering, and 90-degree view rotation;
- interactive 3D preview rotation and zoom;
- editable Property panel and object tree;
- object deletion by the Delete key for supported design objects;
- bounded Undo/Redo history;
- DXF save/load with CosmicDesigner JSON metadata;
- automatic import of general ASCII DXF L/V/V1 geometry and unit conversion to centimeters;
- preservation of imported contour geometry through CosmicDesigner metadata.

## 4. Major data structures

- `CosmicDesignerDocument`: aggregate root for material, outer/inner contours, bend objects, W/H segments, cuts, and micro joints.
- `MaterialModel`: flat width, height, and thickness.
- `BendObject`: axis, direction, position, bend angle, V-cut depth, and residual thickness.
- `SectionSegment`: editable W/H section length.
- `ContourObject` and `GeometrySegment`: outer/inner L geometry represented by line, arc, and circle records.
- `CutOperation`: hole/cut shape, center, size, polygon sides, and generated geometry.
- `DocumentSnapshot` / `UndoRedoManager`: bounded state history.

## 5. Major design principles observed in source

- Domain models and calculation/serialization engines are separated from WPF views.
- Geometry coordinates and material dimensions use centimeters inside CosmicDesigner.
- L represents cut geometry; V and V1 represent opposite bend directions.
- Section segment and bend positions are recalculated together.
- DXF remains interoperable while CosmicDesigner-specific state is embedded as metadata.
- General DXF import does not overwrite the source by default; Save As is required.
- Destructive design edits are expected to participate in Undo/Redo.

## 6. Current known limitations

- Binary DXF is unsupported.
- General import only converts the geometry entities supported by the shared parser.
- No explicit CosmicDesigner semantic version is declared.
- Proposed Recent/View menus, ruler/grid persistence, Flat Designer pan/zoom and direct manipulation, transparent Section dimension editors, and file-persistent Section view state are not established as implemented baseline behavior.
- Git history could not be inspected in this workspace.
