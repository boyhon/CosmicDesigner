# ADR-005 — Regular star cycle identity and material semantics
Status: Accepted, CR-079, 2026-10-07.

Use CR-078 regular circumcircle vertices, connect i to (i+K) modulo N, with N5..512 and K2..floor((N-1)/2). gcd(N,K) cycles belong to one RegularStar Cut with a stable Step parameter. Ordered LINE caches, optional StarStep metadata/history and one closed DXF LWPOLYLINE per cycle preserve all crossing segments. Do not replace the user's intersecting star by an alternating-radius silhouette or add bridges between cycles.

Existing ADR-003 per-Cut even-odd semantics apply to both self crossings and multiple cycles; material islands remain. Flat mask, selection and 3D use the same cycles. Display outlines and DXF retain every crossing line. Internal union splits same-object intersections before boundary classification; nonmanifold unions reject without mutation. Simple contour Outer subtraction and direct corner fillet reject star primitives. A merged object becomes Compound and loses parametric star identity. Manufacturing interpretation of crossings must be reviewed in actual external CAD; this implementation is not manufacturing approval.

Future nonzero/solid-star fill is a distinct user-visible requirement rather than a silent change to current compound semantics. No file schema change or product renaming required; additive metadata is ignored by older readers which may not interpret this new shape correctly.
